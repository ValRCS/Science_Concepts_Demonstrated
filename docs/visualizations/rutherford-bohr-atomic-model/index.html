Rutherford's gold-foil experiment

Alpha particles were directed at a thin metal foil. Most passed through with little deflection; a small fraction scattered through large angles. The observation supported a concentrated positive nuclear charge rather than Thomson's diffuse positive-charge model.

For Coulomb scattering by a heavy, approximately stationary point nucleus, the Rutherford differential cross section is proportional to 1/sin⁴(θ/2). This experiment does not itself establish Bohr's discrete energy levels.
